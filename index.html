AI Research & Products

AI research and products built for a multilingual world

MUXBITE is an independent AI lab dedicated to training capable, safe, and accessible language models — without depending on anyone else's infrastructure.

Explore our models Read our research

MUXBITE is built on hard questions.

We believe the next generation of AI should not be built by a handful of incumbents alone. Language models that serve billions of people should reflect the full diversity of human language — not just English.

We are asking what it takes to train frontier models as an independent lab, starting from the ground up, with limited resources and unlimited intent.
