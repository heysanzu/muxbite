Who we are

An independent AI lab
with a long view.

MUXBITE is an independent AI research company building proprietary language models from the ground up. We believe the future of intelligence should not be owned by a handful of large incumbents — and that language models built for a global, multilingual world require a different kind of founding intent.

Our work spans model research, developer tooling, and consumer products. We are building the full stack — from the weights to the interface — under one roof, with one mission: safe, capable, and accessible AI for everyone who needs it.

MUXBITE operates across three divisions: MUXBITE X (flagship agent platform), MUXBITE Data (B2B infrastructure), and MUXBITE Research (IP and licensing). We are at the early stage, and we intend to stay independent for as long as it takes to do this right.
